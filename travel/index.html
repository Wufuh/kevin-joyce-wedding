Taipei

Travel & stay

Helpful notes for reaching Taipei and lodging near the Mandarin Oriental.

Getting to Taipei

Most international guests arrive at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport (TPE). From there, the Airport MRT, taxis, and private transfers make it straightforward to reach central Taipei.

Songshan Airport (TSA) is closer to the venue for some regional flights.

Staying near the venue

The wedding is at the Mandarin Oriental, Taipei in Songshan District, along Dunhua North Road. Guests who prefer to stay on-site can inquire directly with the hotel about availability.

The Dunhua / Nanjing area also has many nearby hotels within a short taxi or MRT ride of the venue.

Getting around

Taipei's MRT is clean, frequent, and easy to navigate in English. Taxis and ride-hailing apps are widely available. The Mandarin Oriental sits near major roads and MRT connections in eastern Taipei.

Neighborhood notes

Songshan and nearby Zhongshan / Dazhi offer restaurants, cafés, and shopping within easy reach of the hotel. We'll add favorite spots and any group-rate hotel notes here as they become available.
